# Conquer the World: what ships

Version 1.0 is the base game plus the ten content updates below, all in the first release. They are numbered so
they can also be announced one at a time.

## 1.0: the base game

Real-time territory conquest on a chart-style world map. Four eras, doctrine drafts, landmarks, our own art,
font and flags. Solo against the AI on three difficulties, public matches and team games with bots filling the empty seats.

## The ten updates

1. **Doctrines II and Edicts.** The doctrine pool doubles from 12 to 24, with new effects: attack, defence, troop
   regrowth, cheaper claims and building refunds. Every Reckoning doctrine now grants an edict: Windfall, Levy,
   Jubilee, Blitz, Fortify or Muster.
2. **Landmarks II.** Citadels, Harbors, Granaries and Foundries join Mints and Garrisons: 12 sites a map, 6 kinds.
3. **World Events.** Twelve events that shift the match for a minute or two: Gold Rush, Plague, Hard Winter,
   Market Crash, and the Uprising and Tax Revolt that fall on whoever is ahead.
4. **Rivals.** Every nation has a character (Conqueror, Merchant Prince, Warden, Schemer, Zealot) that changes how
   it fights and what it drafts. Nations taunt you, use edicts, and can become your nemesis, with a gold bounty.
5. **Game Modes.** Blitz, King of the Hill, Deadline, Skirmish and Total War beside Conquest. Pick one on the solo
   menu; public matches rotate through the ones that suit a crowd.
6. **New Worlds.** Six invented maps: Primeval, Thousand Isles, Twin Realms, Mare Nostrum, The Crescent, The Spine.
7. **Field Orders.** Three daily and three weekly goals from pools of 24 and 12.
8. **Medals and the Commander's Record.** 50 medals, 20 ranks with insignia, titles to wear, lifetime statistics.
9. **The War Room.** A campaign of 24 missions in 4 theatres, each with three objectives and a star for each.
10. **War Report and Season One.** A chart of your land against the leader's and the match's key moments after every
    match; a free 30-level season track with six titles, four themes in rotation; 14 new territory skins.

Career, orders, medals, stars and the season are cosmetic on purpose: the client reports them, so they never buy
anything and nothing is sold through them.
